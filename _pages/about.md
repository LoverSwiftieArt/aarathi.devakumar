Howdy! My name is Aarathi Devakumar, and I am a multidisciplinary engineering technology (mechatronics) student at Texas A&M University!

I love the mix of mechanical, electrical and software engineering to execute robotic and automation systems! Mechanical + Electrical + Software = Mechatronics (MXET) Engineering at TAMU!

My interests are in robotics, mechatronics, and automation research and development, and I have had a passion in the subject starting from a young age. Ever since competing in middle school robotics with my Girl Scout Troop, I instantly fell in love with the process of designing a robot. I later researched various types of robots and was amazed by how they could help others live a better life and shape the world, and I strive to be part of that process. I aim to discover ways to combine my technical and application experiences in robotics along with various fields such as defense or manufacturing through research, but particularly in fashion design and any creative outlet, designing clothing that is stunning by aesthetics and automation. 

As a woman in STEM, I also aim to empower girls to find an interest in STEM by participating and organizing outreach programs. I currently have an engineering instagram that shares my experiences as a female engineering student: girlypopengineer. The girlypopengineer website will be coming out soon, featuring some of my experiences starting in my major, resources that have helped me, career tips, and how to live a well-balanced life between engineering academics and personal life. 

Outside of robotics and design, I have a background in music performance. I have played piano for 13 years and the violin for 7 years. I continue violin through the Texas A&M Chamber Orchestra and play the piano as a way to relax myself!

Outside of designing robots and machines, you can find me listening to music, playing my piano and violin, taking aesthetic pictures through my Instax mini, traveling, exercising, making delicious food, playing around with vintage electronics and video games, gardening, styling my fits, and just being curious and open to new opportunities and activities.

Feel free to check out my projects and my accomplishments through my portfolio website!
